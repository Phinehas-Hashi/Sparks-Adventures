Sparks Adventures is an emerging travel and experiences brand connecting curious people with local places, landscapes and stories across Kenya. We want adventure to feel welcoming, considered and worth remembering—not complicated or out of reach.

We start with listening, honest budgets and careful planning. Trips only become bookable when the details are checked and the plan is ready.

intention.

01

People before packages

Build trips around real interests, welcoming groups and shared experiences.

02

Local knowledge matters

Listen to local guidance and aim to support the people and places that host us.

03

Clear plans. Clear costs.

Be upfront about what is known, what is included and what still needs checking.

Safety is part of the adventure

Verify routes, transport, access and practical risks before inviting anyone to book.
